# Wildlife next steps

Keep wildlife wordless: movement and habitat changes communicate the state of the valley.

1. **Forest life:** add small groups of agoutis on the ground, marmosets moving between crowns, and parrots landing in trees. Tree clearing triggers flight toward remaining habitat.
2. **River life:** add fish silhouettes in shallow water, frogs on the banks, and more capybara families. Warming and pollution reduce activity before animals disappear or mutate.
3. **Open land:** add armadillos near scrub and anteaters wandering through clearings. Roads and construction push their routes farther from the datacenter.
4. **Habitat behavior:** choose safe dry routes for ground animals, canopy routes for climbers, and perches for birds. Avoid buildings and unfinished construction; use visible pauses, grazing, and social groups rather than constant circling.
5. **Gradual corruption:** slowing, uneven movement, altered posture and color, then distorted silhouettes and horns. Restored trees bring life back before the final irreversible stage.

Start with agoutis and parrots using the tree-clearing escape behavior. Add one species at a time; tune population and habitat preferences in a wildlife config. Reuse simple low-poly geometry and cap active agents to keep the prototype fast. Keep staff transformation and robots for a later pass.
